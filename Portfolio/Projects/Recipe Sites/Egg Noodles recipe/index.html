Quick Egg & Noodles

ポニョ風熱々たまご麺
Savory Stir-Fried Egg Noodles with Crisp Garden Vegetables & Shoyu Umami

Straight out of a cozy Ghibli kitchen scene—evoking the late-night storm noodles in Ponyo and Calcifer's sizzling skillet in Howl's Moving Castle. Tender noodles are wok-tossed with crisp peas, carrots, and sweet peppers, folded with velvety golden eggs, and glazed in a fragrant shoyu sauce.

Ghibli Kitchen Secret: Push your sautéed vegetables to one side before pouring in the beaten egg. Covering the skillet for just 45 seconds lets the trapped steam cook the eggs silky-soft while sealing in the shoyu aromatics!

Pantry Ingredients

  • Egg or fresh ramen noodles 1 cup (dry)
  • Toasted sesame or vegetable oil 2 tbsp
  • Mixed veggies (peas, carrots, peppers) 1 cup
  • Farm fresh egg, beaten 1 large
  • Japanese shoyu (soy sauce) 1 tbsp
  • Sea salt & crushed black pepper To taste

Anime Kitchen Magic

  • High Protein Comfort: Farm eggs provide wholesome energy to fuel your adventures and keep you full.
  • 15-Minute One-Pan Speed: From pantry to steaming bowl in minutes with minimal dishes to clean.
  • Customizable Toppings: Top with green scallions, sliced nori, chili oil, or sesame seeds as you please.

Step-by-Step Preparation

1

Boil & Drain Noodles

Bring a pot of water to a lively rolling boil. Drop in your noodles and cook until tender with a pleasant chew (al dente). Drain thoroughly in a colander and set aside.

2

Stoke the Skillet Fire

Heat 2 tablespoons of sesame or vegetable oil in a wok or heavy-bottomed skillet over medium-high heat until hot and shimmering.

3

Flash-Sauté Garden Harvest

Toss in your cup of colorful mixed vegetables (diced sweet carrots, garden peas, and bell peppers). Sauté briskly for 3 to 4 minutes until crisp-tender and fragrant.

4

Golden Soft-Scrambled Egg

Push the vegetables to one side of the skillet. Pour the beaten egg into the cleared space. Gently scramble with a wooden spatula until softly set and velvety.

5

Toss & Glaze with Shoyu Umami

Fold the cooked noodles into the skillet with the vegetables and eggs. Drizzle in 1 tablespoon of savory shoyu soy sauce, sea salt, and black pepper. Toss vigorously until every noodle is glossy.

6

Plate, Slurp & Enjoy Steaming Hot

Transfer into warm ceramic noodle bowls. Garnish with chopped scallions and sesame seeds, and enjoy your comforting, homemade Ghibli-inspired egg noodles!
